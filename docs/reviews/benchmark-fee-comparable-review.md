# Fee comparable review

- Separate semantic review: `src/domain/benchmark/comparable-review.ts`; no rate extraction, tier math changes or writes to existing fee facts. Its own version is `adv-comparable-v1`; `FEE_VERSION` stays `adv-2a-v8`.
- Evidence: all stored physical pages of the exact primary brochure, including Items 4/5/7; an archived original PDF remains usable if current documents moved on. Whole-review budget is 240,000 characters; missing/unreadable/oversized evidence yields a named unclear result, not a guessed pass. Scope and minimum answers and section coverage each require grounded quotes/physical pages.
- Rules are model categories/prompts, not filing-specific patterns: ordinary individual/family services can be general; named strategies/products, institutional/adviser-channel and cohort restrictions are scoped. Applicable account minimums remain effective when waivable; termination rights are not account minimums.
- Durable run/status tools: `comparable-jobs.ts`, `src/mcp/benchmark-tools.ts`, independent migration/table and worker registration. Idempotent requests, per-firm atomic fact/checkpoint writes, expiring fenced leases and heartbeat support restart. Stale workers cannot commit; quota pauses affect only this review. A paused requestId can resume through its run tool. Old review-version jobs are not silently upgraded.
- Peer protection: `comparable-gate.ts` and `compute.ts`; a current fee-fact/version review is required for exact and numeric two-reading prices. Failed/unclear/missing reviews supply no peer fee values, range percentiles or revenue estimates. Passing intervals remain ranked against exact peers without entering the reference sample. Waiver-dependent balances withhold both readings and retain both endpoints in `conditionalFeeValues.ranges`. Original raw values remain unchanged. Account minimums also gate revenue at average account size.
- `benchmark_report` reports the new revision's comparable-review status counts separately from raw extraction coverage. Historical revisions remain immutable and must be recomputed after reviews before fee comparisons are used.
- Synthetic evidence tests: nine unit and nine MCP cases cover scope/minimum decisions, grounding, stale versions, peer/revenue exclusion, preserved extraction facts/jobs, leases, quota and archived PDFs. Added two-reading pass/fail/unclear/waiver tests, partial-balance waivers and default/explicit selection. Passing ranges get percentile endpoints; failed/unclear/conditional ranges do not.
- Expanded saved-checkpoint population: 804 eligible / 687 core, comprising 548/473 exact-comparable firms plus 256/214 numeric two-reading firms. A pure offline selector check reproduces all 687 core firms; saved benchmark outputs independently reproduce 804 eligible firms. Default and explicit IDs use the same predicate, excluding incomplete one-reading, non-primary and nonnumeric output. No production/provider call was made.
- All firms with saved numeric benchmark fee output can now receive a current review; the exact-only omission is removed. Until reviewed they stay outside new peer numbers; original facts remain intact. The earlier audit found one scope failure and one waiver-dependent firm among 20; actual live outcomes require the owner’s later authorized run.
- Plan details: fail closed for unreviewed as well as unclear inputs in new benchmark revisions. Added one independent job table/migration; no changes to extraction job tables/worker or fee extraction code. No production review, compute, extraction-job read/control or deployment was performed.
- Validation and CI results are recorded in the PR. Only synthetic isolated test databases are used.
