# RIA decisions

- D-69 (2026-10-31): RIAs become a sector; all SEC-registered US advisers imported; large firms and non-wealth firms
  kept aside; two size groups. See `docs/business/decisions.md`.
- 2026-11-08 (owner): firm decisions are Accept, Hold or Reject. Accepted firms are Tier 0 (solo), Tier 1 (2–100
  staff, not Tier 2) or Tier 2 ($500M–$10B and 10–100 staff). Solo firms are now accepted (Tier 0, called first).
  Firms mostly serving funds, companies or pensions are on Hold, not rejected. Reject: too large, outside the US, not
  active, 0 staff. Replaces the Proof (P) and Retainer (R) groups. Tier 2 is called after the US holiday week.
