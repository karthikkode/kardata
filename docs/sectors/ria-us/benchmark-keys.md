Use the registry’s existing `firm` and `financials` keys where definitions match. The existing disclosure flag is text, so the filed Item 11 boolean has a separate key. Snapshot facts use the filing date and release month qualifier.

SEC history ZIP part 1 contains the IA Base A/B, A/B ownership, and D 1.F and 1.I tables. Part 2 contains D 5.K.(3) and 7.A. Join by filing ID and use data through December 31, 2024. Registered-adviser monthly releases for September 2023–2025 are XLSX. The supplied October 2026 ZIP contains `ia09012026.csv`.

Schedule records are `sec_adv_owner` (A/B ownership and officer status) and `sec_adv_schedules` (D 1.F/1.I offices and URLs, 5.K.(3)/7.A custodians). Custodian shares use reported 5.K.(3)(g) dollars divided by 5F total AUM less 5D(3)(d–f). Counts cover reported records. Historical schedule data stops in 2024.

Part 2A Item 5 uses `firm.fee_schedule` with extraction/input qualifiers, `fee_schedule.status`, and the `fee_schedule_terms` record. Tier rates are annual percentage points and balances, minimums, and calculated fees are USD. The record retains dated quotes and uses null for unstated terms. Scenario fees apply the disclosed marginal or cliff schedule and applicable minimum.

`benchmark.fee_comparable_review` is independent of fee extraction (`adv-comparable-v4`): it binds the exact fee fact ID and stored physical brochure pages, reviews general versus asset-class/strategy/product/client-type scope, account minimums and existing-price consistency across Items 4/5/7, and retains dated quotes. `benchmark_fee_comparable_run` queues exact-comparable and numeric two-reading firms with the same check for default and explicit IDs; `benchmark_fee_comparable_status` reports durable progress and decisions. No fee fact or extraction version changes. A new benchmark computation requires a current passing review: failed, unclear and unreviewed inputs have no fee peer numbers or revenue estimates. Passing ranges remain low/high values ranked against exact peers. Waiver-dependent balances retain both endpoints under `conditionalFeeValues.ranges`, outside peer inputs, with raw prices preserved. Saved review population: 804 eligible / 687 core. Historical benchmark revisions are unchanged; recompute after review before using fee comparisons.

The comparable worker retries a transient provider transport failure once after a one-second backoff, rechecking provider readiness. In auto mode, a Muse transport retry uses Codex only when its separate auth/model/quota snapshot is ready and no hold exists; explicit provider modes and global settings remain unchanged. Quota/auth holds remain pending; non-transient and exhausted failures stay visible. Status results include `attempts`, `retryCount`, `providers`, `attemptErrors` and `usageIncomplete`; failed-call token usage is unknown, never invented, and elapsed time includes retry/backoff. Restarted workers retain completed firms and safely resume pending ones. This worker-only recovery leaves v4 review inputs, cached decisions, fee facts and the extraction worker unchanged.

Benchmark peers use AUM bands <$100M, $100–250M, $250–500M, $500M–1B, $1–3B, $3–10B, and ≥$10B, with inclusive lower bounds. The separate group for staff ≤1 takes precedence. Other groups use ≥2/3 and ≤1/3 individual plus high-net-worth regulatory AUM share. Sparse groups merge within that mix with the nearest occupied band, lower band on ties. A group still below 20 has no percentiles or published quartiles. Per-measure sample sizes accompany midpoint percentiles and interpolated quartiles.

Website software uses the sector host map and records dated portal, custodian, scheduling, form, and e-sign link evidence in `website_software`. A successful scan with no matching links is `none_found`. Blocked, unreachable, and missing sites are separate coverage outcomes.

`firm.filed_corporate_website` is a separate JSON fact from `sec_adv_website_promote` (dry run by default): normalized homepage, original 1.I addresses, filing ID/date, brochure cover quote/page/source/date, and promotion timestamp. Exactly one globally unshared, non-provider host must be printed as a website on the latest stored firm-brochure cover. Email-only evidence is insufficient. Company website/domain are untouched. The collector uses this field only when the current company input is missing or shared, and rechecks all stored company/filing host claims before use. Saved-checkpoint expectation: 658 core candidates, subject to the tool's identity and evidence checks; this PR does not promote them.

Comparable review v2 treats diversified/balanced/customized multi-asset schedules and firm-wide styles as general. `net_worth_target` and `relationship_preference` retain cited targets without treating them as account balances; true account/managed-assets minimums take precedence. The assets limb of an assets-or-net-worth requirement remains conditional below that limb. Unread pages are named in the model input: a readable, grounded restriction can fail; every other outcome remains unclear. V1 facts remain stored but cannot qualify a new revision. No extraction version or fee fact changes; re-review and recompute require the later operator go.

Comparable review v3 checks the selected fee table's priced asset coverage before the firm's service description. A table pricing only one asset class or portfolio component is `asset_class` scoped, even when the firm also manages diversified portfolios. Its applicability quote is retained, both exact prices and ranges are withheld from peer inputs and revenue estimates, and raw extraction is preserved. A single schedule covering diversified portfolios remains general. V1/v2 reviews cannot qualify new computations; historical revisions remain unchanged. The v2 47-firm gate found a false pass of a component table, so no full review or new production benchmark revision was run.

Comparable review v4 independently validates supplied exact/two-reading prices and the stored annual-floor input against explicit brochure rules, including annual fee minimums outside Item 5. `pricing.status` is `supported`, `contradicted` or `unclear`, with its own grounded physical-page quote. A grounded contradiction fails the review; uncertain or ungrounded support stays unclear. Rates are not re-extracted, replacements are not calculated, and extraction facts remain unchanged. Waivability does not make a below-floor price unconditional. Current schedules open to new clients are not legacy cohorts merely because they have an effective date; separately priced optional services do not turn a general multi-asset schedule into an asset-class component. V1–v3 reviews remain stored but cannot qualify a new computation.
