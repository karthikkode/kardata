# How we pick a sector

Garden-outdoor was picked from Apollo's industry tags without measuring anything. It cost a day: the list was full of
retail-led manufacturers, dormant sites, sub-brands, PE-owned and too-big companies. It was deleted on 2026-10-28.
Never pick a sector that way again.

## 1. Start from what people buy, not from industry tags
- **Gate:** something consumers buy daily or often and **buy again** (used up or replaced). Skincare works because
  it is part of everyday life, so the market is full of D2C brands.
- **Where to look:** Amazon (what is actually sold and bought), not Apollo's industry list. Apollo is the contact
  book, not the place we choose companies from.
- **Two ways to slice:**
  - by product: beauty, fashion (apparel, footwear, jewellery and more), food (vast), pet care;
  - by buyer group: "what do fitness people buy?", "what do new parents buy?", and so on.
- Durable, bought-once goods sold mainly through retailers (grills, sheds, appliances, most toys) fail the gate.

## 2. Score a random sample before importing
Take about 50 random companies from the candidate and measure:

| # | Measure | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Share that are founder-led brands selling their own products online | Garden failed here |
| 2 | Share selling in 2+ places (own store + Amazon, retailers or wholesale) | That is the pain we sell |
| 3 | Share at roughly $1–25M and 10–100 staff, with no data team | Our tiers; Apollo's figures are checked, not trusted |
| 4 | Share independent (no PE or group owner) and alive (site updated in 6 months) | Garden's dead and owned rows |
| 5 | Share with a reachable founder, COO or ecommerce lead | We call people, not companies |
| 6 | Real prospects left after 1–5 | Under ~300 isn't worth a sector |
| 7 | One pain story fits most of them | One pitch, many calls |

Compare 2–3 candidates in one table. The owner picks. Only then do we import the full list.

## 3. Order
1. **Skincare now** (D-66), despite the holiday-season pressure on them.
2. **Beauty beyond skincare** (haircare, makeup, body care) after skincare: closest fit, most of the pipeline carries over.
3. Then fashion sub-sectors, food sub-sectors and pet care, each scored as above.
