# Evidence rules

Status: locked · Last updated: 2026-10-25 · Source: kardata_old/campaign/email-framework.md (evidence honesty),
problems-brief.md contracts v2/v3, owner call-coaching 2026-10-24

These apply to emails, calls, briefs, case studies and anything the analysis agent says about a company.

## What counts

1. **Quote it, or it isn't evidence.** Every claim rests on one of: a press line, a job-post quote, a dated review
   quote, or a stored source with its quote checked.
   - Star ratings alone don't count.
   - A theme only counts once the review text has been read, not just the snippet.
2. **Date it, or it's stale.**
   - Every cited fact carries a date.
   - Job posts older than 6 months don't count as "open roles".
   - Check again in the week you send.
3. **Behind a login means unknown, never missing.** Never describe what's behind a login, and never claim their
   customers "have nowhere to look".
4. **Read the page, not the status code.**
   - A page that returns "200 OK" can still be an empty shell.
   - A help center that seems closed has often just moved. Check other domains and subdomains before saying
     anything is missing.
5. **No claims about how their product feels to use.** Only quoted, dated reviews from the last 12 months count.
6. **A pattern isn't a diagnosis.** We can say two signals often go together. We can't assert a specific problem
   inside their company without their data.
7. **Absence of evidence is not evidence.** "No jobs board found" or "no reviews found" never props up a verdict.
8. **Solution in motion vs unaddressed.**
   - A job post, a new tool or a new agency means they're already working on it. Don't challenge it. Complement it,
     open somewhere else, or come back later.
   - Open on signals nobody is working on.

## Numbers

- **Every frequency states its sample.** Say "31 of the 152 brands I researched", never "20% of brands".
- **State how the sample was chosen.** A census-based sample is fair but still the outside view. A hand-picked sample
  overstates how often something happens.
- **Outside stats need two independent sources** before they're used anywhere.
- **Internal value estimates are never shown to prospects.**

## Kardata data labels

- **Prefer facts marked `verified` or `verified_value`.** Use `doc_only` as context, with care.
- **Never cite `quote_mismatch` or `unverified` items.**
- **Many findings are "nothing found" statements.** They're useful as gaps but are never the hook on their own.
- **Revenue estimates that come from Apollo are Apollo's,** even when an extractor quoted them. They don't
  independently confirm anything.

## Privacy

- **Reviewer identities are never stored or quoted.**
- **People appear only in their public business roles.**
- **Never name a company negatively in public content** without public evidence anyone can check. When in doubt,
  keep it anonymous.
