## Who we are

- **A solo specialist practice:** Karthik, a data engineer.
- **The work:** data foundations for growing businesses, plus AI **only where it pays for itself**.
- **Not an "AI automation" agency.** Never lead with AI. The work is finding where time and money leak, then closing
  the gaps worth closing.
- **Mission line** (brand only: LinkedIn, website, calls; never in a cold email): "I help companies transition into
  AI."
- **Positioning line** (calls and website):

  > I help growing businesses that run on too many disconnected systems find where they're losing time and money,
  > then close the gaps worth closing.

  - Describe their situation, not a label. Say "brands that sell on their own site and on Amazon", not "Shopify
    brands".
  - Never assume the problem, for example "nobody trusts your numbers". Most teams trust their numbers until
    something breaks.
- **Email signature:** "Karthik / Data Guy, kardata." No slogans.

## The goal

- **1+ paying client within 100 days.** Day 1 = 2026-10-13, day 100 = 2027-01-20.
  - The entry is a paid diagnostic, priced by tier (see `offer.md`). It leads to a build or a monthly retainer.
- **A paid Tier C job ($500k–2M revenue, `offer.md`) also counts.** It covers expenses and gives us the
  first case study.
- **A paid audit counts as real progress on its own.** From outside data, most problems look audit-sized rather than
  retainer-sized. The retainer case usually shows up only once we see their own data.
- **Leading indicators:** replies, calls booked, calls held, audits sold. **Lagging:** a signed retainer.

## Sectors

- **The practice covers many sectors.** Each sector is a starting point, not the identity.
- **Current sector: D2C skincare,** in English-speaking markets (US, UK, CA, AU).
  - **Who qualifies:** a D2C brand (so public data exists) with enough revenue to pay, about $500k a year or more.
    The store platform doesn't matter.
  - **Tiers** group companies by annual revenue and set the offer (A $10M+, B $2–10M, C $500k–2M). **Layers** set priority inside
    a tier by signal score (Layer 1 = highest). Full rules are in `icp.md`.
  - We have deep research on 753 companies (research wave 1) and 156 more in wave 2.
- **Sector rule:** research one sector deeply, prove the method, then move to the next. Research stays **one sector
  ahead of sending, never more.**
- **Known risk for D2C:** margins are thinner than in software. A $3k retainer is about 18% of a $2M brand's profit
  at a 10% margin. The audit-first entry, the $2k Light retainer and the "3× the fee" rule (see `offer.md`) exist for
  this. Watch price objections.

## How we win clients

1. **Outside-in research first.**
   - Kardata collects public data for every company in the sector.
   - We find patterns across the sector and specific signals per company.
   - Everything stays private. Nothing about a named company is ever published.
2. **One narrow opening per conversation.** Lead with one specific, easy-to-understand problem, never the full list
   of services. Current openings:
   - **One true weekly number across channels:** own store + Amazon + retailers + ads.
   - **Retailer and wholesale paperwork into clean data:** purchase orders, PDFs, spreadsheets, photos.
3. **Outreach:**
   - **Cold calls** are for discovery: fast feedback, real concerns, and the owner's strength at questioning.
   - **Cold email** works alongside them, and both are manual.
   - **Content** supports outreach and never replaces it: 90% outreach, 10% content.
4. **The call sells the meeting. The meeting sells the audit. The audit earns the retainer.**

## Standing rules

- **Never compete with a brand's agencies or recent hires,** and never suggest they are slow. Position on scope:
  the gap between systems that nobody owns.
- **Agencies as a referral channel come later,** after 2–3 clients.
- **Opportunity, never fear.** Show what becomes possible. Stakes appear only as honest maths, always paired with
  the upside.
- **Sending comes before research.** Research is the comfortable part and the easiest place to lose days. No new
  research starts while sending is blocked on something else.
- **Proof from a cold start:**
  - sector case studies built on public data;
  - the first 2 audits free, for Tier B Layer 1 companies, in exchange for a testimonial (see `offer.md`);
  - the guarantee from day one;
  - before the first send, a basic online presence: a sharp LinkedIn profile and one case study live on kardata.co.
