# Brief: financials (agent_label = financials)

Answer: who funds and owns {{name}}, and what is credibly known about its money?

Checklist:
- funding_event records (amount, round, investors, date), each citing a reputable source (press, filings, the company itself).
- Ownership as facts: independent, private equity, acquired, or parent company — with the acquirer's name and date where applicable.
- Revenue estimates ONLY with a named source and a source qualifier on the fact. Never estimate revenue yourself; if no credible estimate exists, say so and stop.
- Budgets: about 8 searches, 15 pages. Filings and reputable press only — no aggregator guesses presented as fact.
