# Brief: reviews (agent_label = reviews)

Answer: where is {{name}} reviewed, how is it rated, and what do customers love and hate?

Your deliverable is notes, not structures. An on-site collector already bulk-loads on-site reviews — your job is the platforms it cannot reach plus the themes.

Checklist:
- Which review platforms exist for this company (Google, Trustpilot, Yelp, retail sites, press roundups). Per platform note the rating, the review count and the URL, each with [src:<uuid>].
- Read widely: at least 3 sources for this area. Quote review text only, never with reviewer names or initials.
- Distill the top 5 positive and top 5 negative themes with example quotes, and write reviews/themes.md plus reviews/summary.md with a ratings-per-platform table. Every theme, number and quote carries [src:<uuid>].
- Note anything surprising: fake-review suspicions, rating cliffs after a date, themes that contradict the marketing.
- If you cannot reach 3 sources, end honest: area_status_set not_found listing what you tried.
- Budgets: about 8 searches, 15 pages. This area usually needs the browser first — coordinate with the main agent so only one area uses it at a time.
