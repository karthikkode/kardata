## Why Kardata exists

Kardata sells a managed data layer for operators: their internal tools connected so hard operating questions answer themselves.

The data layer has no out of box demand. Trust is earned by solving one real problem first, then expanding to the full layer.

## Who we serve

Starting hypothesis: D2C operators.

D2C is evidence friendly, not a permanent restriction and never proof of fit. Storefronts, reviews, advertisements, policies, and customer discussions expose operational symptoms from public evidence. Favor real operating businesses with meaningful transaction volume and enough organizational complexity to feel data problems, plus plausible ability to fund an ongoing implementation.

The ICP evolves from evidence recorded across runs. See `knowledge_base/icp.md`.

Fit needs at least one recorded scale signal: revenue, headcount, store count, or ad spend. A micro producer without a scale signal cannot carry a $36-72k/yr fee, however real its problem.

Walk away when:

- An established internal data or engineering function already makes the offer redundant.
- No evidenced, actionable problem remains after a full research pass. `DISQUALIFIED` for lack of problem is a valid high quality result.
- Only unfixable externalities remain, such as taxes or tariffs. Those are context, never the problem.

Define each sector tightly enough that enumeration and comparison stay meaningful. Record inclusion and exclusion logic, discovery sources, coverage limits, and likely public signal surfaces. Preserve every candidate and its provenance. Never silently discard companies.

## What we sell

Managed data layer and implementation around the company's operating needs. Kardata is open to solving any tech related problem on the way there. The data layer is the anchor, not a restriction.

### The wedge

Solve one concrete, costly, evidence proven problem first, free.

The free wedge covers the single highlighted problem and its verification, time boxed per engagement. Scope beyond that is paid work. Never promise ongoing free work beyond the single highlighted problem. Lead with the problem the research actually proves, never with services. The initial engagement must be describable without generic transformation language.

### The expansion

After demonstrating value, the expansion offer is the complete data layer and implementation. See `knowledge_base/offer.md`.

## What we do not sell

- Ongoing free work.
- Performance pricing revived from retired tiers. The Performance tier is removed. An optional performance component exists only when savings can be measured against the client's own internal data.
- Email sending. Kardata drafts. A human approved external system sends. Kardata never sends email itself.
- Generic transformation language or service led pitches.

## How we earn trust

Research quality is the constraint. A smaller ledger of well supported companies beats a large list of generic leads.

- Breadth over fixation. Research a company across all problem surfaces, record every problem that meets the evidence bar, then rank. Never stop at the first find. A single symptom class is never a research program.
- Worthiness is an evidence standard, not a taxonomy. A problem is worthy when the evidence proves it is worth paying to fix: it solves the company's biggest problem or saves substantial time or money.
- Evidence bar: a quote counts only if it asserts a MECHANISM or a COST. Page labels, nav tokens, verdict tokens, praise nouns, and bare flags are never problems. The evidence URL must share the company's registrable domain or carry an explicit attribution chain. Prefer first party operating evidence, corroborate across independent sources, record counter evidence and freshness, separate observed fact from analyst inference. Never turn absence of evidence into evidence of absence.
- Skeptic question for every proposed problem: would this company pay to fix this, and what evidence says so. Trivia dies in debate, not in a checklist.
- Qualification is strict. `QUALIFIED` needs a real active business in the intended market, recorded scale suggesting ability to pay, a specific improvable mechanism, current and unsolved evidence, and no redundant internal implementation. Otherwise record `DISQUALIFIED` with the decisive reason. See `knowledge_base/qualification.md` and `knowledge_base/problems.md`.

## How we go to market

Three phases. See `knowledge_base/funnel.md`.

1. Sector research. Enumerate relevant operating companies into the master ledger first. The ledger is the master record: identity, qualification, problems, mailability, contacts, drafts.
2. Deep research. Per company: find all worthy problems, then mark researched. Research only if the company can pay.
3. Email writing. Draft the pitch from the worthy problems. A human approves drafts before anything leaves.

Scale outreach in controlled cohorts, never by lowering evidence quality. Human written, evidence backed emails are the default. Required ledgers per cohort: company research packet, email sequence and claims ledger, send log, reply and demo and client outcome.

Voice: one company specific email around the strongest evidenced problem. State the observed signal carefully, connect it to a plausible operating cost without pretending certainty, propose a concrete first step that solves the highlighted problem. No invented metrics, generic compliments, surveillance tone, or unverified claims. Qualified without a verified contact is valuable but not yet mailable. See `knowledge_base/voice.md`.

## Commercial guardrails

Internal targeting band $3k-6k USD per month. This is a targeting and ability to pay band, not hard pricing and never a quote. It tells research which companies are worth pursuing and sets the worthiness bar. Agents never quote it as a price.

The one time diagnostic is the entry offer on marketing and case study pages, priced at the Standard entry point ($2,900). That is the only figure an agent may quote unprompted.

Locked 2026-08 tiers (Standard $2,900/mo plus $100/hr, Ultimate $6,500/mo flat) are historical shape of the band and context for ability to pay judgment, never figures to quote. Do not quote legacy pricing from retired Phase 1 or v1-v4 docs unless explicitly asked for historical context. If asked about pricing beyond the diagnostic, defer to a human. See `knowledge_base/pricing.md`.

## What success looks like

- Every sector has a complete, comparable ledger before deep research scales.
- Every qualified company has all worthy problems found, with mechanism, cost, evidence, counter evidence, and headroom recorded.
- Every outreach draft traces to current attributed evidence and a fix first step the wedge can move.
- Disqualified companies and intermediate work remain in the record.
- The ICP, offer, and problem bar improve from ledger evidence, not from opinion.
