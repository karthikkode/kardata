# Market notes: regional home-services rollup

Collected 2026-03-14 after visiting 42 shops across the metro area. All
figures below are owner-reported unless marked verified.

## Demand signals

- Average ticket on the surveyed routes is $3,200, up 12.5% year over year.
- Earliest booking backlog starts 2026-06-01; 7 shops are already full
  through that week.
- Callback rate sits at 96 per 1,000 jobs. Best shop holds 31 per 1,000.
- Owners report 1,240 unread quote requests sitting in shared inboxes.
- Last full audit of these numbers: 2025-11-30.

## Pricing bands

Typical retainers run 3-6k USD per month for multi-crew operators. Single
crews cluster near 48 hundred per month all-in. Crews start dispatch at
09:30 and run 2,000 billable hours per tech per year at 15% overtime.
