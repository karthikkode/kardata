# Offer

Kardata sells a **managed data layer** for operators: their internal tools
connected so hard operating questions answer themselves.

## The wedge (entry)

Solve one concrete, costly, evidence-proven problem first — **free**. The
free problem is the way in: the data layer has no out-of-box demand, so a
closely-related solved problem earns the trust and operating access that a
cold data-layer pitch cannot. The initial engagement must be describable
without generic transformation language.

Default bound: the free wedge covers the single highlighted problem and its
verification, time-boxed per engagement. Scope beyond that is paid work.

## The expansion

After demonstrating value, the expansion offer is the complete data layer
and implementation around the company's operating needs. Kardata is open to
solving any tech-related problem on the way there; the data layer is the
anchor, not a restriction.

## Rules for agents

- Lead with the problem the research actually proves, never with services.
- Never promise ongoing free work beyond the single highlighted problem.
- Research quality is the constraint: a smaller ledger of well-supported
  companies beats a large list of generic leads.
