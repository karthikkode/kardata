# Funnel (three phases)

1. **Sector research.** Enumerate relevant operating companies into the
   master ledger first. The ledger is the master record: identity,
   qualification, problems, mailability, contacts, drafts.
2. **Deep research.** Per company: find all worthy problems, then mark
   researched. Research only if the company can pay.
3. **Email writing.** Draft the pitch from the worthy problems; a
   human-approved external system sends. Kardata never sends email itself.

## Operating rules

- Scale outreach in controlled cohorts, never by lowering evidence quality.
- Human-written, evidence-backed emails are the default; automation only
  after claim validation, number provenance, and sender tracking are
  reliable.
- Required ledgers per cohort: company research packet, email sequence
  and claims ledger, send log, reply/demo/client outcome.
- A human approves drafts before anything leaves. Research proposals are
  not canonical company mutations.
