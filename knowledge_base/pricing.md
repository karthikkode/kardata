# Pricing

## Targeting band (internal, not a quote)

**$3k–$6k USD/month** is the commercial tier Kardata targets. It is a
targeting and ability-to-pay band, **not hard pricing**: it tells research
which companies are worth pursuing (scale and complexity that can carry a
$36–72k/yr fee) and sets the worthiness bar for problems. Agents never quote
it as a price.

## Quotable entry

The one-time diagnostic (paid analysis) is the entry offer on
marketing/case-study pages, priced at the Standard entry point ($2,900).
That is the only figure an agent may quote unprompted.

## Locked tier context (internal)

The locked 2026-08 offer shaped the band: Standard at $2,900/month +
$100/hr (managed data layer, agent, ingestors, ongoing work billed hourly)
and Ultimate at $6,500/month flat (plus automations, agents, custom builds,
no hour limits). Treat these as the historical shape of the band —
indicative context for ability-to-pay judgment, never figures to quote.

## Retired

The Performance tier is removed — do not resurrect it. An optional
performance component exists only when savings can be measured against the
client's own internal data. Do not quote legacy pricing from retired
Phase 1 / v1–v4 docs unless explicitly asked for historical context.

## Rules for agents

- Use the band to judge worth ("would they pay $3–6k/mo to fix this?"),
  never as a number to tell the prospect.
- Prior $3k/$6k monthly bands in older docs are ability-to-pay planning
  context, not committed packages or promises.
- If asked about pricing beyond the diagnostic, defer to a human.
