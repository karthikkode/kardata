# Qualification

A company is `QUALIFIED` only when all are supported:

- It is a real, active business within the run's intended market.
- Its scale and complexity make the problem commercially meaningful and
  suggest ability to pay (recorded scale signal required).
- Evidence supports a specific mechanism Kardata can improve, not merely
  a generic symptom.
- The problem is current enough to act on and not clearly solved already.
- The engagement is not obviously redundant with a capable established
  internal data implementation.

Otherwise record `DISQUALIFIED` with the decisive reason. Lack of a
contact affects mailability, not qualification.

## Decision record

A qualification decision must identify: the problem(s), mechanism,
evidence, counter-evidence, and why the wedge offer is credible. Every
clause above is a rejection rule. Disqualified companies and all
intermediate work remain in the record.

## Research depth

Research only companies that can plausibly pay. Once engaged, find **all**
worthy problems before marking the company researched. Qualified companies
without a verified contact remain valuable but are not yet mailable.
