# ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

## Current hypothesis: D2C operators

D2C is the starting hypothesis because storefronts, reviews,
advertisements, policies, and customer discussions expose operational
symptoms from public evidence. Favor real operating businesses with
meaningful transaction volume and enough organizational complexity to feel
data problems, plus plausible ability to fund an ongoing implementation.

D2C is evidence-friendly, **not a permanent restriction and never proof of
fit**. The ICP evolves from evidence recorded across runs.

## Fit signals (record at least one scale signal)

Revenue, headcount, store count, or ad spend — enough scale and complexity
to make the problem commercially meaningful and suggest ability to pay.
A micro-producer without a scale signal cannot carry a $36–72k/yr fee,
however real its problem.

## Kill signals (walk away)

- An established internal data/engineering function that already makes the
  offer redundant.
- No evidenced, actionable problem after a full research pass —
  `DISQUALIFIED` for lack of problem is a valid high-quality result.
- Unfixable externalities (taxes, tariffs) are context, never the problem.

## Sector discipline

Define a sector tightly enough that enumeration and comparison are
meaningful. Record inclusion/exclusion logic, discovery sources, coverage
limitations, and likely public signal surfaces. Preserve every candidate
and its discovery provenance; never silently discard companies.
