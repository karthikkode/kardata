# Outreach voice

Write one company-specific email around the strongest evidenced problem.
State the observed signal carefully, connect it to a plausible operating
cost without pretending certainty, and propose a concrete first step that
solves the highlighted problem.

The broader data-layer implementation is an expansion path, not an opening
dump of services. Avoid invented metrics, generic compliments,
surveillance tone, and unverified claims.

Mailability: a usable, appropriately sourced outreach contact is required
before a draft becomes sendable. Qualified without a verified contact is
valuable but not yet mailable.
